Voice Signal TTS 0.1.0

Initial NVDA addon release from Blazy Enterprises.

Four original Motorola V3c voices: US English, Mexican Spanish, Canadian French,
and Brazilian Portuguese. Includes native rate control, adjustable pitch and
volume, nvwave playback, accented text, language switching and cancellation.

Install the attached voiceSignalTTS-0.1.0.nvda-addon, restart NVDA, and select
Voice Signal TTS with NVDA+Control+S. Requires 64-bit Windows and NVDA 2026.1+.
The existing Motorola V323i addon can remain installed.

Packaged synthesis and automated driver-boundary tests passed. Live NVDA
validation is not yet recorded. This independent addon is not an official
Motorola, VoiceSignal or Nuance release. Bundled firmware and third-party
components retain their respective rights and licenses.
